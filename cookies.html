This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how We use them, the types of cookies We use, and your choices regarding these technologies.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on Your computer, mobile device, or any other device by a website. They are widely used to make websites work or function more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies:

  • Persistent Cookies remain on Your personal computer or mobile device when You go offline. They are used to remember Your preferences over time.
  • Session Cookies are deleted as soon as You close Your web browser. They are essential for navigating the website during a single visit.

3. How We Use Cookies

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes set out below:

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Type: Session Cookies
Administered by: Us
Purpose: These Cookies are essential to provide You with services available through the Website and to enable You to use some of its features. They help to authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services that You have asked for cannot be provided.

Analytics and Performance Cookies

Type: Persistent Cookies
Administered by: Third-Parties (e.g., Vercel Analytics)
Purpose: These Cookies are used to track information about traffic to the Website and how users use the Website. The information gathered may include Your device's IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on those pages. We use this privacy-conscious aggregated data to improve Our platform's performance.

Functionality Cookies

Type: Persistent Cookies
Administered by: Us
Purpose: These Cookies allow Us to remember choices You make when You use the Website, such as remembering your form inputs or visual preferences. The purpose is to provide You with a more personal experience and to avoid You having to re-enter your preferences every time You use the Website.

4. Third-Party Cookies

In addition to Our own cookies, We may also use various third-party plug-ins and services embedded on this website to report usage statistics or deliver media. Services such as external maps, YouTube or Spotify video/audio embeds, and social media content may place their own cookies. These cookies are governed by the respective providers' privacy and cookie policies, and We do not have direct control over how these third parties utilize their tracking technologies.

5. Managing Cookies

If You prefer to avoid the use of Cookies on the Website, You must first disable the use of Cookies in Your browser and then delete the Cookies saved in Your browser associated with this website. You may use this option to prevent the use of Cookies at any time.

Please note that if You do not accept Our Cookies, You may experience some inconvenience in Your use of the Website, and some features (such as embedded media or persistent form data) may not function properly.

  • For the Chrome web browser, please visit this page from Google: Clear cache & cookies
  • For the Safari web browser, please visit this page from Apple: Manage cookies
  • For any other web browser, please visit your web browser's official support pages.
